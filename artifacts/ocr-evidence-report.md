# OCR pipeline evidence report

Date: 2026-09-01

## Bottom line

The repository now has a modular schema v2 pipeline with swappable literal readers and ordered region stages. Its main design property is evidence preservation: specialist text can support, challenge, or abstain, but it cannot silently erase the original text, geometry, alternatives, or failures.

The strongest completed claims are narrower than the final goal:

- the eligible Nemotron candidate beats the failure-inclusive Tesseract CPU floor on ClinOCR transcription;
- the docTR and OSD guard selected a minimum-CER cached view on all 56 frozen rotated pages, with no aggregate guarded-latency claim;
- Microsoft Table Transformer is strong on a 60-table public detection and structure panel;
- geometry-first tri-source fusion repaired all five reviewed Nemotron cell errors on a targeted two-table panel;
- the tested generic handwriting specialists failed clinically clear fields and were rejected;
- the expanded 44-page private hard run routes every page to review, while manual inspection still finds no complete page.

The system has not yet beaten a frozen hosted frontier baseline. A fresh inherited OpenRouter key and exact provider pins are absent, so direct and paired hosted comparisons remain externally blocked.

The paired ClinOCR intervals resample 16 template clusters with 10,000 bootstrap draws at seed 0; they are not repeated inference seeds. Missing, failed, invalid, and abstained cases are scored in every stated denominator.

## Manual review and rejected routes

- Both financial tables and every remaining cell disagreement were inspected at original resolution. The tri-source rule changed exactly five Nemotron cells, and each replacement matched the image.
- The one PubTables detector false positive was a 9.6 pixel clipped neighboring fragment. The valid table still localized at IoU 0.970220.
- Three representative Heron pages showed text over-segmentation, missed titles, and missed table captions.
- Twelve handwriting crops and four Doc-UFCN overlays were inspected. Clear identifiers, medication names, and numbers were wrong, while the detector also flooded printed labels and form rules. No evaluated handwriting route was integrated.
- All 44 private hard pages were inspected. Orientation recovered the five rotated pages, but handwriting, dense-grid controls, table relationships, faint text, and multi-pane reading order remained incomplete. The final evidence-risk contract routes all 44 pages to review instead of silently accepting them. The two added application screenshots retain their page-sized false-table proposals as rejected evidence while preserving literal OCR.

These reviews diagnose failure mechanisms. They do not replace the fixed public denominators or justify unmeasured promotions.

## Origin and license boundary

| Component | Origin and license | Current decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tesseract | Apache-2.0 | CPU floor and local fallback |
| NVIDIA Nemotron OCR v2 | NVIDIA Open Model License | Eligible literal-OCR candidate, not a frontier winner |
| Microsoft Table Transformer | Microsoft, MIT | Current eligible table detector and structure arm |
| IBM Heron | IBM release, Apache-2.0 | Research-only because of RT-DETRv2 lineage |
| Teklia PyLaia and Doc-UFCN | France, MIT | Rejected on measured quality |
| Microsoft TrOCR base | Microsoft model, MIT stated on a third-party-authored card; IAM-derived | Rejected on quality; commercial data-lineage review unresolved |
| Mindee docTR orientation | Mindee code, Apache-2.0; torchvision MobileNetV3 | Current orientation proposal; weight-specific license review remains |
| NVIDIA Nemotron Parse 2.0 | NVIDIA, OpenMDW-1.1 plus tokenizer terms | Adapter retained, handwriting route rejected |
| Paddle, GLM, Qwen, DeepSeek, MinerU, and derived systems | Chinese-origin model or backbone lineage | Public-data research comparators only |
| Meta Muse Glimmer and Google Gemma | Non-Chinese candidate lineages, Apache-2.0 releases | Hosted repair candidates, still unmeasured here |

Private clinical images stay local or on the authorized private A10G. They are not sent to Jina, OpenRouter, or other hosted services.

## What remains before the end goal

1. Run frozen, same-page hosted baselines and the routed candidate with exact provider pins and a fresh inherited key.
2. Measure the complete eligible stack end to end, including failures, abstentions, p50 and p95 latency, peak GPU memory, and cost per attempted page.
3. Replace the research-only layout arm and unresolved handwriting route only after a fixed panel of at least 30 cases improves without transcription regressions.
4. Fine-tune only after stage-oracle analysis shows a model bottleneck. The current evidence does not justify adapting a 30B model first.
